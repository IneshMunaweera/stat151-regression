Try it yourself

  1. Change the strength. Turn on “Show residuals” and move r² from 0 to 1. What happens to the vertical gaps between the points and the line? What happens to SSE?
  2. Change the direction. Set r² to 0.64. Before switching from positive to negative, predict what will happen to the slope, r, and r². Check your prediction.
  3. Reverse X and Y. Predict grade from exam time, then use “Reverse X and Y” to predict exam time from grade. Read both slope interpretations aloud. Which statistics stay the same?
  4. Change the scales. Put exam time on X in hours and grade on Y as a percentage. Switch hours to minutes. What happens to the slope? Now switch the grade to points out of 50. What happens to SST, SSR, SSE, and r²?
